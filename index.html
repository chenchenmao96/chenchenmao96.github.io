Selected research

Outer Limits

Research infrastructure · N = 219 · Accepted at CSSSA 2026

An open-source Chrome extension and Express/MongoDB backend for controlled experiments inside live Old Reddit. It replaces posts and comments, logs interactions, and contains participant actions so they never reach the host platform.

Instrument validation combined ART ANOVAs, exploratory equivalence tests at a d = ±0.50 bound, and qualitative coding with intercoder reliability above .80 to assess whether the manipulation introduced detectable artifacts.

How Far Do Capability Cues Travel?

Anthropomorphism and differentiated trust · N = 270

A randomized 2 × 2 field experiment on Reddit tested functional breadth and explanation. The breadth display changed perceived breadth—the only randomized main effect to survive correction—while explanation had no reliable effect despite 75% recognition.

Perceived intelligence was associated with ability, integrity, and benevolence trust; the incremental association of anthropomorphism was specific to benevolence. These findings motivate evaluating the specific judgments an interface is designed to change and measuring trust as separate dimensions.

When Readability and Source Retention Diverge

An evaluability gap in AI translation · N = 306

A 2 × 2 comparison in TransLingo examined source-text condition and output rendering, alongside an audit of readability and source retention. Participants saw both source and translation, but holistic quality ratings tracked retention differences only for simple texts.

For complex prose, outputs with sharply different source overlap received nearly identical ratings. The study identifies a condition in which human quality judgments may fail to register fidelity loss, and models how perceived quality relates to intelligence, anthropomorphism, trust, and disclosure willingness.
